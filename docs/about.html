About this site

Home & Rental DIY Ideas is a small home & DIY publication focused on updates that work for renters and small spaces: no drilling, no permanent changes, and a clear path back to move-out condition.

Every guide is researched from real product listings, manufacturer specs, and publicly available customer reviews before it's written. Where a claim comes from other people's real-world experience (installers, reviewers, other DIY writers), we say so directly in the text rather than presenting it as our own untested experience.

We link to specific products because we've evaluated them against the criteria described in each guide, not because of a paid placement. Some of those links are affiliate links — see our disclosure page for details.

Questions or corrections: if something in a guide is out of date or incorrect, we want to know. Reach out via the contact details on this site once they're live.
